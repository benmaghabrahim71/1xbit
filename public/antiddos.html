How PletX Works

Unlike traditional protection systems, PletX focuses on adaptive filtering and efficiency, meaning your services stay online and responsive even during attacks.

1. Traffic Routing & Filtering

All incoming traffic is automatically routed through PletX protection layers. Suspicious or malicious packets are blocked instantly, while legitimate users are allowed through.

2. Smart Protocol Detection

PletX analyzes traffic patterns in real time and automatically detects protocols such as:

Game servers (FiveM, Minecraft, SA-MP), Voice services (TeamSpeak), VPN connections (WireGuard, OpenVPN), Remote access (SSH, RDP)

3. Advanced Filtering Technology

Using technologies like XDP and eBPF, PletX processes traffic at extremely high speed with minimal resource usage, ensuring maximum performance and efficiency.

4. Real-Time Monitoring

The system continuously monitors incoming & blocked traffic, attack patterns, and bandwidth usage. This allows instant response and automatic mitigation.
